Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Shorts Maker ("the app") is a personal tool used by its developer to create short videos and publish them to the developer's own social media accounts.

What the app accesses

When the owner links a YouTube account, the app uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos that the owner chooses to publish to the owner's own channel. The app requests the permission needed to upload videos and nothing else.

What the app stores

Login tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's device only. The app has no server and does not send any personal data, tokens, videos or account information to the developer or to any third party, other than to the platform (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram) the owner is publishing to and to Google Gemini for transcription and caption writing of the owner's chosen videos.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal data.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from the Google Account permissions page: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest revision.
